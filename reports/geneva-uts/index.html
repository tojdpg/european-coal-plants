What This Demonstrates

Urban Thermal Sinking studies how Geneva stores, releases, and ventilates heat across concrete, lake, rivers, parks, tree canopy, and dense urban form. The research question is economic as well as physical: do cooler micro-climates, lake access, parks, shade, and ventilation corridors line up with wealth, real-estate value, and lower energy demand? This is a hypothesis to test, not a finding yet. The current system does not claim a finished street-level prediction model. It demonstrates a controlled pipeline that can gather, align, validate, and gate the evidence needed before a weather-conditioned model or operational PINO is justified.

Current Gate

Passed for demo evidence. ECOSTRESS authenticated ingest, station evidence, Clay context, Landsat LST, SUEWS pilots, and a Torch neural-operator sandbox are present.

Blocked for scientific claims. No PET labels, no 2 m citywide dynamic air-temperature labels, no SUEWS flux calibration, and no operational PINO or validated neural-operator claims are authorized.

Evidence Ladder

The project separates each evidence role so the demo remains inspectable. Recognition models, teacher fields, observations, and physics pilots do not collapse into one opaque claim.

Research question

Thermal-Wealth Hypothesis

The first proxy map colors building fabric by likely cool-value or heat-burden conditions. The final analytic target is to test those candidates against parcel value, rent, income proxies, building type, and expected cooling demand per square meter.

Official modeled teacher

SITG / FITNAH

Scenario-clean modeled fields provide the canonical P0 teacher layer for heat-trap, air-temperature, PET, wind, and cold-air-flow context. These are not called observational truth.

EO representation

Clay / Sentinel-2

Clay supplies local satellite context and embeddings for selected hot events. It is semantic and spatial context, not physics and not a thermal label.

Surface thermal observation

Landsat and ECOSTRESS

LST scenes provide surface-temperature evidence. They help inspect thermal patterns, but they do not replace 2 m air temperature or PET.

Point weather validation

MeteoSwiss / GExplore

Station and probe records provide temporal weather context for hot events. They are point evidence, not a 10 m citywide validation field.

Physics pilot

SUEWS

Local physics runs test surface-energy behavior and counterfactual cooling potential. The pilot is useful for screening, not calibrated public evidence.

Dynamics sandbox

PINO / Neural Operator

A Torch Fourier-operator run demonstrates runtime dynamics-tool integration on Apple GPU. It is trained on synthetic proxy dynamics, not observed Geneva labels.

Control layer

Evidence Gate

Publication, model-training, and claim escalation require explicit gate checks: provenance, scenario discipline, target semantics, uncertainty, and validation.
