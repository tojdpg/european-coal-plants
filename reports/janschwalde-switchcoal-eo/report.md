# SwitchCoal + Earth Observation: Janschwalde

This Quarto report tests the SwitchCoal 100 x 100 km replacement-zone idea
against an Earth-observation resource stack for Janschwalde power station.

## Assumption

Coal generation could potentially be replaced by a nearby renewable mix within
roughly a 100 x 100 km search area while reusing or building on existing energy
infrastructure.

The 100 x 100 km frame is a search box, not a proposed renewable build
footprint.

## Current Evidence Layers

- ESA WorldCover 10 m 2021 v200 land-cover classes.
- PVGIS PV potential and irradiation samples.
- Open-Meteo / ERA5 2023 daily mean 100 m wind-speed samples.
- Water and storage context based on land cover and proximity.
- TEE viewport link for the smaller plant-footprint interpretation window.

## Tentative Interpretation

The first pass points toward a selective hybrid replacement logic rather than a
single technology answer. A large part of the 100 km frame remains low or
constrained in this screen. Solar, wind, mixed solar-wind, and storage-context
areas appear as pockets to investigate, not as a ready-made replacement plan.

## Caveat

This is not a feasibility model. It does not yet include official
protected-area masks, settlement buffers, land ownership, slope/aspect, grid
capacity, grid queue status, turbine setback rules, permitting risk, financing,
or project economics. OpenStreetMap grid/water/protected context overlays were
unavailable in the current build, so official grid and protection layers remain
pending.
